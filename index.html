With Care & Grace

Special considerations

A gentle word on menstruation, pregnancy, married couples, and taking part in the Homa — speak with us quietly and we'll guide you with love.

Dress Code

Dressed
with devotion

  • Sarees are encouraged at the Temple — especially the last three days. Otherwise, a ¾ kurti and scarf is fine around Parthi.
  • Saree-tying ladies will be available on the last three days.
  • Please bring safety pins.
  • Men — long pants and a shirt.
Temple Etiquette

Entering the sacred

  • Please wash your feet before entering the Temple.
  • Sarees on the last three days.
  • When seated near the Sanctum, remain still and silent.
The Programme

Nine evenings
of devotion

  • Evening programme on days 1–9: the bathing and dressing of Maa, and devotional songs & bhajans.
  • The Homa takes place on the last day only, from 8am to 11am.
  • On Dasera day, lunch is offered after the Homa.
